Sam Sinai (سام سینائی)

Algorithmic searchfor physical novelty

I’ve spent the past decade building an understanding of the structure of natural solution spaces and algorithmic approaches to find novelty within them. I studied computer science and AI at MIT and earned my PhD in Mathematical Biology at Harvard. I co-founded Dyno, where we build AI systems that design capsids that would never be found through natural evolution. If we succeed, a new field of medicine will become reality, and people will be able to have agency over their genetics.
